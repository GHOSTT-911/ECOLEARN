Sustainability Basics

Sustainability is the practice of meeting our own needs without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs. It is built on three pillars: economic, environmental, and social. By balancing these three, we can create a healthier world for everyone. Sustainable practices are essential for combating climate change, protecting biodiversity, and ensuring a stable global society.

Renewable Energy

Renewable energy is derived from natural resources that are replenished constantly. These sources include sunlight, wind, rain, tides, waves, and geothermal heat. Unlike fossil fuels, which are finite and release greenhouse gases when burned, renewable energy sources have a minimal environmental footprint. Adopting renewable energy is crucial for combating climate change and building a cleaner, more sustainable future.

Waste Management

Waste management involves the collection, transport, processing, recycling, or disposal of waste materials. The most widely used approach is the 'waste hierarchy,' which prioritizes prevention over disposal. The cornerstone of this system is the 3Rs: Reduce, Reuse, and Recycle. By reducing our consumption, reusing items, and recycling what we can, we can significantly lessen our impact on landfills and incinerators.
